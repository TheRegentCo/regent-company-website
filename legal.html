Accessibility report

Last reviewed: 8 October 2026. This is an internal self-assessment for transparency, not an independent WCAG certification.

The public website has been reviewed for keyboard navigation, visible focus, responsive reflow, form labels, heading structure, mobile navigation, readable type and core colour contrast. The site includes a skip link, visible focus states, labelled form controls, an ARIA live region for enquiry status, responsive layouts and reduced-motion support.

Known limitations: a full independent WCAG 2.2 AA audit and a complete VoiceOver/NVDA/JAWS test matrix have not yet been completed. Long legal pages may also benefit from additional document navigation.
