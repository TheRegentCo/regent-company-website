Regent Mile

Business delivery built around London.

Regent Mile is The Regent Collection's last-mile delivery venture, being developed to support reliable courier work for London businesses through clear dispatch, tracking and account-based operations.

Register business interest →Back to ventures
London business courier

A delivery network designed for recurring commercial work.

The Regent Mile model is being built around businesses that need dependable local delivery rather than one-off consumer demand alone. The initial focus is London, with particular attention to East London and Tower Hamlets.

01

Business courier work

Local collection and delivery workflows intended for retailers, bakeries, offices, professional services, hospitality operators and other businesses with repeat delivery needs.

02

Same-day delivery

Time-sensitive delivery is part of the intended service model, with dispatch and driver tools designed to make collection, tracking and completion easier to manage.

03

Business accounts

Regent Mile is being developed with recurring company accounts in mind, including negotiated pricing, company users, controls and invoicing as the platform matures.

Service areas

London first, with an East London starting point.

The planned launch strategy is deliberately focused. Building density in a defined area can support faster dispatch, more predictable courier availability and better economics before wider expansion.
