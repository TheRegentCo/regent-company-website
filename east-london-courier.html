Regent Mile · East London

A courier network being built from East London outward.

Regent Mile's initial operating strategy centres on East London, where local business density, short urban routes and repeat delivery relationships can create a strong foundation for wider London coverage.

Why East London

Local knowledge can be an operational advantage.

Regent Mile is intended to grow from a part of London where the business understands the local geography, merchant mix and practical delivery challenges.

01

Shorter operating radius

A concentrated service area can make courier positioning and repeat routes easier to manage than trying to cover all of London from day one.

02

Business relationships

The commercial strategy prioritises direct relationships with local companies that may need regular delivery support throughout the week.

03

Scalable technology

Dispatch, tracking and business account tools are being built so the operating model can expand without losing visibility or control.
