A venture development company built to turn practical opportunities into real businesses.

Who we are

Venture development is our core corporate role.

The Regent Collection Ltd is a British venture development company. We identify credible opportunities, shape clear propositions and bring together the operating, commercial and digital foundations needed to build a functioning business.

Regent Mile is our current publicly presented venture. Other opportunities and internal development work remain private until they are ready to be described.
