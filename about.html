Company

A modern operating company with room to grow.

The Regent Collection exists to build and operate businesses that combine practical service, strong execution and useful technology.

Who we are

One company. Multiple areas of opportunity.

The Regent Collection Ltd is the parent company behind a growing portfolio of service-led activity. Our model is deliberately broad: identify a real commercial need, build a focused solution, operate it responsibly and expand only when the foundations are strong.

Our registered business scope currently includes non-store retail, car and light-vehicle leasing, and combined office administrative activities. Alongside that corporate scope, Regent Mile is being developed as our technology-enabled last-mile logistics venture.
