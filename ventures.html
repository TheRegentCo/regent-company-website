Current venture
Regent Mile

Regent Mile is The Regent Collection’s London-focused last-mile delivery endeavour. It is being built around a courier network, dispatch and tracking technology, business delivery accounts and flexible vehicle options for different delivery needs.

The immediate strategy is B2B-led: winning recurring delivery work from London businesses and building dependable local density. The longer-term product direction includes customer ordering, merchant participation and a broader marketplace experience.

The Regent Mile direction

Start with delivery operations. Build outward from a strong core.

The venture is deliberately phased so that the operational network and business relationships can mature before broader marketplace expansion.

Phase 01

Courier network

Reliable dispatch, driver tools, tracking, proof of delivery and operational controls for real delivery work.

Phase 02

Business accounts

Recurring delivery relationships, negotiated pricing, company users, controls and invoicing for business customers.

Phase 03

Marketplace

A customer and merchant layer for placing and accepting orders once the delivery infrastructure is ready to support it.
