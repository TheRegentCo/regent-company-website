Ventures

Different opportunities. One development discipline.

The Regent Collection can develop ventures for clients, with commercial partners or within the Regent group. The structure depends on the opportunity and the agreed scope.

Client Ventures

Built for a client.

We research, structure and develop a venture, service or commercial division for a founder or established business under a defined development scope.

Partner Ventures

Developed together.

Where there is a strong strategic fit, Regent may work within an ongoing commercial relationship with a partner around the development and growth of a venture.

Regent Ventures

Developed internally.

The Regent Collection may also develop selected opportunities within the group where the commercial case and operating capability justify doing so.

Engagements may be structured around a development fee, an ongoing commercial relationship or, in selected cases, more direct participation. Nothing on this website is a promise or offer of investment.
