Regent Mile · Same-day

Same-day delivery for time-sensitive London jobs.

Regent Mile is being developed to support local deliveries that need to move quickly, with dispatch, route visibility and delivery confirmation designed into the operating model.

Urgent local movement

Built for jobs where waiting until tomorrow is not practical.

Same-day delivery can matter when stock is needed urgently, a customer order must be completed quickly or a business cannot afford a delay between collection and handover.

01

Urgent collections

For items that need to be collected quickly and moved directly to a local destination without entering a multi-day parcel network.

02

Business-to-business

For stock, equipment, documents or supplies moving between business locations across London within the same working day.

03

Customer deliveries

For local businesses that want a faster delivery option for eligible customer orders where speed can improve the service experience.

Operational design

Visibility matters as much as speed.

The Regent Mile platform direction combines courier dispatch with clearer delivery information so a business can understand what is happening without chasing every job manually.

Dispatch

Structured assignment

Jobs are intended to carry clear pickup, drop-off, order reference and timing information from the point they enter dispatch.

Route

Live job visibility

Tracking and route information are part of the product roadmap to help businesses and operations teams follow active deliveries.

Proof

Delivery confirmation

Completion controls, delivery codes and proof workflows are being developed to create a stronger record of successful handover.

For repeat users

One urgent job is useful. A dependable process for the next fifty is more valuable.

Regent Mile's longer-term business model is not limited to ad-hoc same-day bookings. The service is being designed to support companies with repeat requirements through business accounts, negotiated pricing, user controls and invoicing.
