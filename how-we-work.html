How we work

Make the next decision before building the next thing.

We keep the process simple. Each stage should answer a real business question or produce something the venture needs.

1. Understand

What is the idea, who is it for and what problem does it solve?

2. Test

Check the market, customer demand, pricing and main risks.

3. Plan

Decide what the business needs to sell, deliver and support the service.

4. Build

Create the brand, website, app, systems and operating process that are actually required.

5. Launch

Make sure sales, operations and customer support are ready before going live.

6. Improve

Use real results to fix weak points and decide what to build next.

Not every project starts at step one, and not every idea should reach launch.
