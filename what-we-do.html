What we do

Help to test, build and launch a new venture.

You can bring us an early idea, a new service for an existing business, or a venture that is already being built.

1. Research and business model

We look at the customer, market, competitors, pricing, costs and revenue model. The aim is to find the weak points before they become expensive.

2. Brand, product and systems

We shape the offer and build the parts the business actually needs, such as a brand, website, mobile app, internal system or operating process.

3. Operations and launch

We plan how the service will run, how customers will buy, what suppliers or partners are needed and what must be ready before launch.

4. Growth and improvement

For a live business, we can review pricing, processes, automation, new services and expansion options.

How work is scoped

Pay for the work you need.

Some jobs start with a £495+ Venture Assessment. Others have a clear build brief from the start.

Any larger development work is quoted separately once the scope is clear.
