Regent Mile for business

Delivery accounts designed around recurring commercial work.

Regent Mile is being developed with business customers at the centre of the model: repeat delivery requirements, account-level controls, negotiated pricing and clearer operational visibility.

Business-first model

For companies that need delivery more than once in a while.

The strongest Regent Mile use case is recurring demand. The business product roadmap is built around making repeat delivery easier to manage, price and review across a company.

01

Negotiated pricing

Repeat customers may need pricing that reflects route patterns, volume, service expectations and the type of vehicle required rather than a one-size-fits-all retail rate.

02

Company users & controls

The roadmap includes multi-user business accounts, delegated roles, spending controls and approval workflows for organisations with more than one person booking deliveries.

03

Monthly invoicing

For eligible commercial relationships, the intended direction includes consolidated billing and account reporting rather than requiring a separate card payment for every job.

Operational visibility

More useful than a receipt after the job is finished.

Businesses often need to know what is happening while a delivery is active. Regent Mile's technology roadmap is designed around clearer job status, tracking and delivery records.

Live

Delivery status

Active job information is intended to make it easier for staff to see whether a courier is assigned, collected or approaching completion.

Proof

Completion records

Order references, proof workflows and delivery confirmation can give businesses a stronger audit trail than informal messaging alone.

Control

Account administration

The business layer is intended to support company-level visibility over users, deliveries, spend and recurring operational activity.
